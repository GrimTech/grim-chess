**Important caveat:** no chess training method reviewed here has randomized-controlled-trial evidence of superiority over alternatives. Method files therefore separate *documented facts* (structure, history, outcomes of named players) from *efficacy claims* (mostly expert consensus or anecdotal), and they link methods to the general learning science in [../01-learning-science/](../01-learning-science/README.md).
